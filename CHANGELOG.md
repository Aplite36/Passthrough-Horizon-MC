# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Pre-release: it builds and passes its tests, and has not been play-tested in the game yet.

- Right-clicks pass through wall signs, wall banners, item frames, glow item frames and paintings to the block behind.
- Sneaking gives the normal click.
- A sign keeps the click when you hold dye, an ink sac, a glow ink sac or honeycomb.
- One switch for each decoration in Options > Mods > Click Through > Settings.
